Projects & Research

Dual-Arm Robotic Workspace Tidying System

Bimanual SO-101 system that detects loose tools with YOLOv8m + RealSense and returns them to designated bins, automating the "Sort" step of industrial 5S. MoveIt IK planning paired with per-arm ACT-trained grasp policies. (Mar 2026 to present, team lead, UW TECHIN 517)

MuSHR: Full-Stack Autonomous Navigation

Full-stack self-driving system on NVIDIA Jetson Orin + ROS. Implements particle-filter localization (LiDAR + odometry), Halton-sampled roadmap with Lazy A*, Dubins Path planning, and MPC real-time control. (Oct to Dec 2025)

BuddyBot: Intelligent Delivery Robot

ROS2-based human-following robot on Turtlebot3 using YOLO + MediaPipe for real-time tracking and gesture-driven FSM control.

TACT: Tracking Asking Cost over Time for Personalized Clarification

Robots facing ambiguous instructions must decide whether to ask or act, and prior work treats the human cost of asking as a constant. TACT learns this cost as a function of user state (emotion, time, habits) from implicit interaction feedback, never querying users about their preferences, and distills it into a DPO clarification policy with a lightweight head over a frozen language model. Evaluated on a ground-truth-controlled benchmark and ten simulated users.

Jan 2026 to present, first author. Manuscript in preparation. Code

Algorithm Structure for Real-time General Game Playing Agents

General Game Playing studies AI agents that play unfamiliar games by reading only the rules at runtime, with no human intervention. Prior work has focused on turn-based games using generic heuristics or Monte Carlo/UCT search, but real-time games now dominate the market. This paper evaluates whether existing GGP algorithms can be adapted to real-time play and proposes a dual-server (strategy + acting) architecture to close the gap.

2022, independent research. Paper. My contribution: Sole author. Conducted the literature review and proposed the dual-server architecture, improving breadth and efficiency to better mimic human performance.

WM-Verdict: Benchmarking the Reliability of World-Model Verdicts about Policies

A benchmark testing whether world models correctly predict policy success, pairing imagined rollouts with simulator ground truth from identical start states. I lead the experiment design and built the shared evaluation infrastructure. Aggregate estimates track ground truth, while per-start verdicts are much less reliable.

Sep 2026 to present, Research Assistant, UW. Advisor: Prof. Luyao Niu.
